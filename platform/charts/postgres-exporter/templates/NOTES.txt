THE ONE CHECK THAT MATTERS. In Grafana -> Explore -> prometheus:
  pg_up
  -> 1 means the database credential works. A pod that is Running with pg_up 0
     has mounted the vault successfully but cannot log in: either the `monitoring`
     role was never created, or the vault password does not match it.
